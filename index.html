Percent of each knob’s travel. A and B rise from 0 to their start level over the ramp time; multi-adjust jumps straight to its value and stays there (empty the box to leave it alone). Anything that raises A or B later stops at the maximum.

Modes the Driver can choose

Untick any you don’t want them to pick. Audio modes need something plugged into the box’s audio input. Split and the User modes aren’t offered. The box’s own level limits still apply, and A and B stay within your maximums in every mode.

The mode the box switches to when you start. You can change it again while running.

Self drive

After the ramp up, A and B each go up 1% every interval until they reach their maximum.

Remote Drive

Someone else moves A and B from a link you send, up to your maximums. You approve them first and can STOP at any time.
